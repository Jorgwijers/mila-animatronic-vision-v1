# Snapmaker Innovation Fund Phase 2 — Mila Project Summary

**Project name:** Mila — Animatronic Vision V1  
**Funding type:** U1 Hardware Sponsorship  
**Category:** Other  
**Open Source Status:** Closed Source  
**Originality:** Original New Idea  
**Status:** Concept and active software development

Mila is an independent AI assistant project being developed in the Netherlands. I am working toward a primarily local assistant with an expressive physical desktop presence.

The proposed first hardware build, **Mila — Animatronic Vision V1**, is a modular 3D-printed animatronic head with two moving camera-eyes, blinking eyelids, articulated eyebrows, a movable jaw for future speech animation and a pan/tilt neck.

Rather than a simple decorative shell, the head requires many interconnected moving parts: eye gimbals, camera holders, eyelid linkages, servo brackets, structural joints, removable face panels and serviceable electronics compartments. The intended design has a mechanical inner frame and replaceable printed exterior sections so that components can be tested, maintained and upgraded individually.

## Why a Snapmaker U1?

I want to use the U1 to prototype the mechanically demanding components and explore its multi-color/toolchanging workflow for facial panels and visually distinct assemblies. Repeated print iterations are essential for checking clearances, tolerances, durability, fit and serviceability.

The core technical challenge is making compact expressive mechanisms that can be repeatedly printed, assembled and maintained—not simply printing a display model.

## Proposed development

1. Finalize CAD architecture, clearances and approximate dimensions.
2. Print the modular frame and replaceable panels and test fit.
3. Prototype camera-eye gimbals and eyelid mechanisms independently.
4. Develop eyebrow and jaw mechanisms and verify safe travel.
5. Build a pan/tilt neck and integrate the planned electronics step by step.
6. Connect validated mechanisms to Mila's primarily local software and document all tests.

A provisional electronics architecture includes an ESP32-class controller, a PCA9685-class servo driver, servos, two camera modules and LED rings, with Mila's AI software running on a separate computer. Component choices and motion ranges remain to be validated.

## Value for the maker community

I intend to publish real build photos, design changes, failed experiments, measurement results, relevant printing settings and selected shareable non-proprietary components when practical. This may help other makers developing robotic eyes, articulated mechanisms and modular enclosures.

**Current status:** Mila software development is underway, but this **physical animatronic has not been built**. The accompanying illustration is a concept rendering rather than evidence of working hardware. I do not currently own a Snapmaker U1. Hardware Sponsorship would enable testing and documenting the transition from concept to a physical prototype.
